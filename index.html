About StoryCreator

Creative storytelling made for Android.

StoryCreator is a creative Android application for people who want to build their own stories and animated scenes without needing traditional animation software.

Users can create scenes, place characters and objects, choose character actions, add voice and audio elements, apply effects, and export their finished video.

The goal of StoryCreator is to make digital storytelling easier to start and accessible from a mobile device.
