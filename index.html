About StoryCreator

StoryCreator is an Android application designed to help users create their own stories and animated scenes without requiring professional animation software.

Features

  • Create scenes and stories
  • Characters and character creation
  • Worlds and backgrounds
  • Props and vehicles
  • Character animation
  • Voice and audio
  • Music and effects
  • Video export
